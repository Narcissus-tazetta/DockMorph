First public release.

- Replace any app's icon with a PNG or GIF, and keep it after app updates.
- Experimental: animate the Dock tile with a GIF while the app is running.

Apple Silicon only, tested on macOS 27.2. DockMorph is not notarized: on first launch, choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Accessibility permission has to be granted again after each update.
